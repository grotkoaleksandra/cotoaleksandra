MY NAME IS Ola

Hi, I’m Aleksandra Grotkowska, Ola for short: a digital designer, web designer, video editor and art director based in Warsaw. I work with galleries, magazines and small brands, and on my own projects.

Also: journalism, and things made by hand.

Favourite film: Donnie Darko, 2001.
